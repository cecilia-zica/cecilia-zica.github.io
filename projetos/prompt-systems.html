The problem

A good answer from a model is easy once. The hard part is the same quality on the hundredth run, by a different person, without rewriting the prompt each time.

What I built

I write and maintain instruction systems instead of one-off prompts: a validator that checks event-mapping reports against the product playbook and the dbt macros; a panel of five specialised graders that review an assignment from different angles and have to justify every deduction; and a written house standard — palette, typography, word limits per element, which format fits which occasion — that any model follows to produce a consistent deliverable.

Result

The output stops depending on who wrote the prompt that day. Each system states its rule, its format and the evidence it must show.
