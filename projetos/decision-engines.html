The problem

Eligibility, lead-classification and offer rules lived in people’s heads and in slide decks. Nobody could prove what a rule change would do before a customer felt it.

What I built

I mapped the rules of Itaú, Bradesco, Banco Pan, Banco BV, Banco Safra, Porto Seguro Bank and Santander, wrote each one as logic in DecisionRules, and led the migration to Python and Machine Learning. Every rule got a test case.

Result

408 scenarios under automated test — 292 for eligibility, 116 for lead classification. A rule change is now provable before it reaches a customer.

Screens

A real rule, redacted: the columns and the evaluation order are visible, every threshold is blurred. The cut-offs are the bank’s.
