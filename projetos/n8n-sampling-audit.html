Sampling audit automated in n8n

16 workflows turned 4 hours of weekly spreadsheet work into 5 minutes.

Franq Open Banking2026
20hreturned per month

The problem

The weekly quality sample of AI-read documents was built by hand in spreadsheets — four hours every week, and the sample was never quite comparable to the last one.

What I built

16 n8n workflows wiring BigQuery, Google Sheets and Gmail, with stratified sampling by document type so rare cases always appear. The reviewer receives a sheet that is already filled and an e-mail saying it is ready.

Result

5 minutes a week instead of 4 hours — about 20 hours a month returned to the team, with a sample that is comparable week over week.
