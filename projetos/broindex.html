Broindex — AgroTech sensor research (CNPq)

Time series, data quality and optimisation over 300k+ rows per batch of IoT sensor data.

PIBIC/CNPq · UFSC2025 See the code
300k+rows per batch

The problem

IoT sensors in poultry houses produce more data than anyone can read, and badly placed sensors quietly produce the wrong answer.

What I built

Statistical analysis in R over temperature and humidity series: automated reading and normalisation, anomaly detection and optimisation algorithms for sensor positioning.

Result

Visual analytics on the thermal and physical stress factors linked to higher mortality — research still in progress.
